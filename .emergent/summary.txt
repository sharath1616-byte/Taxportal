The goal is to build a secure, multi-tenant client portal for tax and bookkeeping professionals.

Initially, the product was envisioned as a pixel-perfect clone of Assembly.com, but this was deemed too complex. The current vision is a lightweight, simplified portal that prioritizes core functionality over heavy design aesthetics.

**Core Features (Implemented/In-Progress):**
*   **User Roles:** Separate experiences for Tax Professionals and Clients.
*   **Secure Authentication:** User registration, login, and role-based dashboard access.
*   **Document Management:** Secure document upload with categorization and file validation.
*   **Communication:** A messaging system for client-professional interaction.
*   **Invoicing:** Functionality for professionals to create and manage invoices.

**Newly Requested Features:**
*   **Invitation System:** Tax professionals must invite clients; clients cannot sign up directly.
*   **Email Sync:** Integrate email to centralize communication.
*   **Payment Gateway:** Allow professionals to connect their own accounts (e.g., Stripe/PayPal) to receive payments for invoices.
*   **Expanded Content:** Add Pricing and About Us pages.
*   **Expanded Services:** The portal must support both Tax Filing and Bookkeeping.
*   **White-Label Solution:** Offer a white-label version of the portal as a premium feature.
*   **Enhanced Security:** Implement Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) and reCAPTCHA on forms.
